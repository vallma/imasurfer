Our story

Two seasons, one garage.

We live two hours from a reef break and two hours from a chairlift. For years that meant a garage with boards leaning against every wall, waxed rails against bare concrete, and one of them getting stepped on every November.

Every rack we could buy was made for one sport. Surf racks too shallow for a snowboard's waist, ski racks that crushed a surfboard's rail. So we welded our own out of 3mm marine steel and lined the arms with cork. Neighbours asked for one. Then their shops did.

Cut, welded and powder-coated in Catalonia. The cork comes from Palafrugell, 60km from the workshop.
